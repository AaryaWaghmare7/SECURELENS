Quick FAQ

A few questions people usually have

Why is “uncertain” helpful?

Because some images are genuinely ambiguous. Returning uncertainty is more honest than inventing confidence.

Can I track old uploads?

Yes. Your results, confidence, and image stats are saved to your account history.

Is the detector perfect?

Not yet. The new code is safer than before, but the strongest next step is still a proper labeled evaluation set.
